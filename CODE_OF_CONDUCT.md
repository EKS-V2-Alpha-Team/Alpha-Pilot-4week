# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights and contributions of everyone, regardless of age, body size, caste, disability, ethnicity, experience level, family status, gender identity and expression, language, nationality, neurodiversity, physical appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, socio-economic status or any other characteristic.

## Encouraged Behaviour

We agree to:

1. **Respect the purpose of the project**: stay on topic and help keep discussions productive.
2. **Engage kindly and honestly** with others, in reviews, issues and team channels.
3. **Respect different viewpoints and experiences**, even when we disagree.
4. **Take responsibility** for our actions, contributions and their impact.
5. **Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully**, focusing on the work, not the person.
6. **Repair harm** when it happens, by acknowledging it and making things right.
7. **Look out for each other** and the well-being of the team.

## Restricted Behaviour

We agree not to:

1. **Harass** anyone, or ignore someone's request to stop.
2. **Attack someone's character**, through insults, put-downs or demeaning comments.
3. **Stereotype or discriminate** against anyone based on who they are.
4. **Make sexual comments or advances**, or share sexual content.
5. **Share private information** about others, such as contact details or private messages, without their permission.
6. **Threaten or endanger** anyone, or encourage others to do so.
7. **Behave in any other way** that threatens the well-being of the team.

### Other Restrictions

- **Misleading identity**: do not impersonate anyone or misrepresent who you are.
- **Failing to credit sources**: credit the work, code and ideas of others, including AI-generated content.
- **Promotional material**: do not post ads or self-promotion unrelated to the project.
- **Irresponsible communication**: do not share or repeat restricted behaviour, even as a joke or a quote.

## Reporting an Issue

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it promptly to the project maintainers.

Maintainers will take every report seriously, review it fairly and keep the reporter's identity confidential. Where it is safe to do so, they will be open about how the issue is being handled.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

Maintainers will respond based on how serious the behaviour is and whether it has happened before. The focus is on repairing harm, not only on punishment.

| Level                       | When                                          | What happens                                                           | How to repair                                    |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **1. Warning**              | A single incident                             | A private, written warning from a maintainer                           | Acknowledge the harm and apologise               |
| **2. Limited activity**     | Repeated incidents                            | A warning plus a short break from interacting with the people involved | Reflect on what happened and rejoin respectfully |
| **3. Temporary suspension** | A pattern of incidents or a serious violation | A temporary removal from the project, with conditions for returning    | Meet the conditions before returning             |
| **4. Permanent ban**        | Severe or ongoing violations                  | Permanent removal from all project spaces                              | Not possible                                     |

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, code reviews and team channels. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public.
